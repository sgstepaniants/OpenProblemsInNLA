# MF-22 proof-source architecture

This guide describes the complete candidate source. It does not assert full Lean acceptance, independent referee approval, Comparator acceptance, or publication. The exact reviewed mathematical boundary remains in `STATEMENT-FREEZE.json`; `IMPLEMENTATION-MAP.json` gives every export's location and source hash.

The complete result is eventual polynomial conditioning of the original complex block Toeplitz family for every fixed positive real parameter. The original coefficient blocks and 80-fold scaling are literal definitions. The sufficient formal exponent is two. No bounded parameter interval, omitted exceptional parameter, or finite list of dimensions substitutes for the original quantifiers.

1. `Norms` and `SourceBounds` connect entry estimates to the genuine complex Euclidean operator norm and bound every entry of the original matrix.
2. `Coordinates`, `SourceRows`, `LeadingBlock`, and `SourceRecurrence` derive the literal transfer recurrence from the finite Toeplitz system. They retain the three left boundary values and the single right boundary value; no terminal condition on the other component is added.
3. `ScalarCertificates`, `RootCubic`, `RootCayley`, and `Roots` classify all four actual roots. A complete negative-discriminant cubic argument handles the generic parameter. The separate quadratic and root-at-minus-one argument covers rho squared equal to ten. The root ordering records two unit-modulus roots, one inside, and one outside.
4. `TransferCertificates` computes the genuine transfer characteristic polynomial and the generating denominator and numerator. `Coprime` proves that the two scalar polynomials have no common zero using exact quadratic elimination. All denominators have explicit nonvanishing proofs.
5. `ProjectorPolynomial` and `ProjectorAlgebra` use actual Lagrange polynomial projectors and Cayley–Hamilton. `ProjectorRank` proves the rank-one boundary sandwich through simple-root multiplicity and eigenspace dimension, without presuming diagonalizability or a nonzero boundary entry. `EigenEvaluation` and `Dominant` prove the boundary entry is nonzero by projecting the actual singular-pencil adjugate on both sides, using cyclic trace, then invoking the proved generating-function coprimality.
6. `ScalarTail` and `SpectralTailBounds` bound all nondominant natural powers and normalize the actual boundary scalar. Geometric growth supplies an eventual index, uniformly thereafter. There is no numerical search over the dimension.
7. `GreenKernel`, `StateReconstruction`, and `GreenInverse` establish the exact finite Green formula, reconstruct every source state of its action on every right-hand side, and prove it is the actual two-sided inverse. The first and last states are included. The factor 80 is explicitly restored for the original matrix.
8. `GreenCoefficients`, `GreenEntryAlgebra`, and `GreenEntryBounds` cancel the exponentially growing term before estimating. The remaining five terms involve only fixed four-by-four or four-by-two products and ratios of powers with ordered natural exponents. Every Green entry has one bound independent of all running indices and dimension.
9. `Conditioning` obtains uniform bounds on every entry of the original inverse. The two full entry-to-norm estimates on exactly 2n coordinates give the positive quadratic constant. Singular matrices have infinite condition number in the definition; actual eventual invertibility is proved before that definition is used.
10. `Solution.lean` imports only the implementation and requests kernel-only LeanCert trust plus an axiom print for all 22 frozen exports. The separate Challenge environment is never imported into the proof.

The source uses exact algebra and dimension-independent reductions to avoid interval subdivisions or growing determinant expansions. LeanCert is used for kernel trust enforcement throughout and for the isolated rational certificate in `ScalarCertificates`. Standard foundational axioms are permitted; proof placeholders, added mathematical axioms, unchecked native evaluation and unsafe certificates are not part of the implementation.
