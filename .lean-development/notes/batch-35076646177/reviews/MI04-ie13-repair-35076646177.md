## Mathematical and API review

The pinned Mathlib `Subspace.lean` definition (lines 80–82) makes
`OrthogonalFamily` a pairwise assertion about two actual vectors and the inner
product of their isometric inclusions. Therefore introducing `j k hjk v w` and
changing the goal to the ambient complex inner product preserves the complete
assertion. Submodule inner products are inherited from the ambient space; the
inclusion maps in the statement send each subtype vector to that same vector.

The pair index is deliberately ordered as `Eigenvalues B × Eigenvalues A`, and
`jointEigenSpace A B j` is `eigenspace A j.2.val ⊓ eigenspace B j.1.val`. The
proof's `hv` and `hw` recover exactly those two memberships. Since `j ≠ k`, at
least one component differs. `Subtype.ext` justifies passing from inequality of
eigenvalue subtypes to inequality of their scalar values; there is no assumption
that distinct proof witnesses imply distinct values.

If the first components differ, the two vectors belong to distinct eigenspaces
of `B`, so `hB.orthogonalFamily_eigenspaces` gives the required ambient inner
product in the order `v,w`. If the second components differ, the same argument
uses `A`. The pinned theorem in `Spectrum.lean` (lines 101–109) has exactly this
pointwise application interface. The existing joint-space theorem in
`JointEigenspace.lean` (lines 61–68) uses the identical eigenvalue-component
disjunction, confirming the A/B orientation. The new proof applies the
one-operator theorem to explicitly packaged subtype vectors instead of asking
Lean to identify two whole dependent families.

No commutativity premise is needed for orthogonality alone; the unchanged
spanning lemma still requires and uses commutativity. Multiplicities stay inside
the eigenspaces, so repeated eigenvalues cause no missing case. Zero eigenvalues,
zero vectors, empty intersections, and the helper's zero-dimensional case do not
require additional exclusions. Neither simple spectrum nor invertibility nor a
dimension cutoff has been introduced. The unchanged final construction still
returns an orthonormal basis for the full space and both eigenvalue lists.
