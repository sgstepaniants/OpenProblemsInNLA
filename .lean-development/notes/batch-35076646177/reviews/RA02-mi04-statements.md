## Exact states, histories, and all-rank lower bound

I checked both `arrowheadState` formulas and the full state/update contracts. Before selecting the distinguished label, an ordinary pivot subtracts d_i alpha_i^2 from the corner and removes its row and column. Selecting the distinguished label produces `diag(d)-bb*/c(U)`. Afterwards its i-th ordinary pivot is `d_i c(U.erase i)/c(U)>0`; the next rank-one correction replaces c(U) by c(U.erase i). The denominators remain positive even for empty U, since epsilon^r>0. Selected rows and columns are exactly zero. The negative rank-one state's PSD property follows, for example, from weighted Cauchy–Schwarz with residual factor epsilon^r/c(U). Thus the positivity and update contracts are feasible for every U, including the empty state.

For every distinct history of length at most r+1 the claimed remaining-label state is correct. At length r precisely one label remains. Multiplying the actual pivot values by the final trace gives `epsilon^r * product d_i`: ordinary pivots contribute their d_i, while the distinguished pivot and subsequent c-ratios telescope. Dividing by the actual positive prefix traces gives precisely the `pathContribution` identity. This argument includes the distinguished label first or last, and the alternative where it is never selected. No determinant surrogate, approximate probability, or assumption about all nonprincipal minors is present in the definitions.

The carried-label encoding uses two distinct unselected choices at step s: s and the carry, which is either the last label or below s. If the bit chooses the carry, the carry becomes s; otherwise it remains. Therefore the exact selected set is `initialLabels.erase carry`, differing bit sequences yield different ordered histories, and all encoded histories are distinct-label paths. The contracts include s=0, s=r and the auxiliary r=0 empty history. They justify exactly 2^r retained paths without enumerating a factorial space.

For each s<r, the proposed positive trace bound has the correct direction. With F={s,...,r-1}, the ordinary geometric sum divided by epsilon^s is at most `1+(r-s-1)t^2`. If the last label remains, its weighted and final terms contribute at most `(r-s)t^2+t^2`. If the last label was selected and j<s is the carry, cancel its exceptional diagonal first. Discarding the nonnegative subtracted sum and bounding c(U) below by d_j alpha_j^2 gives the stated upper estimate. The scaled exceptional power has exponent `2(r+1)(r-s)-2(j+1)>=4`, and each remaining weighted term is at most t^2. Consequently both cases satisfy the looser common bound `1+(2r+1)t^2 <= 1+1/r`. Rank one only has s=0 and the first case; empty geometric remainders at s=r-1 are harmless. Selected zero rows add nothing to trace.

The prefix trace product and diagonal product use exactly the same factors epsilon^s, so direct cancellation yields the proposed per-path contribution. Summing 2^r distinct retained paths in the full normalized expectation is valid because every omitted contribution is nonnegative.
